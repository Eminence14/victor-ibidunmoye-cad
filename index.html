About

Most of what I design takes work off someone's hands — a pounder that replaces hours of pounding by arm, a pump that lifts water where there's no power to run one, a grinder that turns on gravity alone.

That's the part I care about: machines that hold up where the grid doesn't, and that a local workshop can actually build. So I take every design through to the assemblies, tolerances and GD&T it needs in SolidWorks — a drawing that can't be made solves nothing.

I learned that on the floor, fabricating aluminium to tight tolerances at Aseyori Ventures and repairing industrial machinery at Polar Machines before I ever specified either. Ten projects here, and I'm still building.
